What everydeck does today

  • Shows every error in plain words, with a ready prompt for your coding agent to fix it.
  • Lists your users, what they did and where they came from.
  • Lets you see and edit the data in your app, like a Shopify admin for any app.
  • Emails you when something breaks, and again when it works.
